📊 Training Effectiveness Data Analysis & Dashboard
📌 Project Overview

The Training Effectiveness Data Analysis project focuses on analyzing employee training programs to understand participation, performance, completion, satisfaction, and overall training effectiveness.

The project transforms raw training data into meaningful insights using Power BI, helping organizations understand how effectively their training programs are performing and where improvements can be made.

🎯 Objectives
Analyze employee participation in training programs.
Measure training completion and attendance rates.
Evaluate employee performance after training.
Understand employee satisfaction with training programs.
Identify high-performing and low-performing training areas.
Analyze training effectiveness across departments and categories.
Create an interactive dashboard for HR and management decision-making.

📈 Dashboard Features
1. Training Overview

Provides a high-level view of overall training activities and performance.

2. Employee Training Analysis

Analyzes employee participation, completion, attendance, and performance.

3. Training Program Analysis

Examines different training programs based on participation, scores, satisfaction, and completion.

4. Department Analysis

Provides department-level insights into employee training and effectiveness.

5. Satisfaction Analysis

Analyzes employee feedback and satisfaction levels related to training programs.

6. Performance Analysis

Helps understand the relationship between training participation and employee performance.
